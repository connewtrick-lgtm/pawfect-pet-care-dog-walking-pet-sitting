About Pawfect Pet Care

Who we are & what we do

Pawfect Pet Care is a small, local pet care service based in Rackheath, Norwich. We're run by Aleksandra — a lifelong animal lover with hands-on experience caring for dogs, cats, rabbits and small animals — and we cover Norfolk and most of Suffolk.

We are fully DBS-checked, fully insured, and pet first aid trained. Every service we offer is built around your individual pet's routine, temperament and needs — not a generic, one-size-fits-all package. Whether your dog needs a quiet solo walk away from traffic, your cat needs her medication at the same time every day, or your puppy needs midday visits while you're at work, we tailor everything to them.

Our services include dog walking (solo and small-group), dog sitting, cat sitting, puppy visits, home visits, drop-in visits, feeding visits, overnight pet sitting, house sitting, and pet taxi. We also offer a specialist Doggy Weddings & Events Chaperone service for dog-friendly weddings and celebrations across Norfolk and Suffolk.

Before any walks or visits begin, we always arrange a free meet & greet so we can meet you and your pets, discuss routines, and make sure everyone is comfortable. No commitment, no pressure — just a chance for us all to get to know each other.

I treat every pet I look after as if they were my own — with patience, kindness and genuine love.
